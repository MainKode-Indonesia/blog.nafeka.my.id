## About the Project

KataKode is a technology-focused blog designed to publish articles, tutorials, and educational content related to programming, web development, software, and the digital world.

The website serves as a place to learn, explore new ideas, and discover useful information about technology. Its content aims to make technical topics easier to understand for readers with different levels of experience.

With a clean interface, readable typography, and a responsive layout, KataKode focuses on delivering a comfortable reading experience across desktop and mobile devices.

As part of the Nafeka digital ecosystem, KataKode complements the other projects by focusing on knowledge sharing and technology-related content.

## Features

- **Article Publishing** — presents technology articles and educational content.
- **Article Detail Pages** — provides dedicated pages for individual articles.
- **Content Navigation** — helps readers browse and discover published content.
- **Readable Layout** — prioritizes typography, spacing, and content structure.
- **Responsive Design** — adapts the website to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **SEO Foundations** — supports search engine discoverability through page metadata and structured URLs.
- **Accessible Interface** — aims to provide clear navigation and a comfortable browsing experience.
- **Ecosystem Integration** — connects KataKode with the broader Nafeka digital ecosystem.

## Design Philosophy

KataKode is guided by the following principles:

- **Clarity** — make articles easy to read and understand.
- **Accessibility** — create an experience that works across devices and screen sizes.
- **Simplicity** — keep the interface focused on the content.
- **Consistency** — maintain a cohesive visual identity throughout the website.
- **Discoverability** — organize content to help readers find relevant information.
- **Knowledge Sharing** — encourage learning through useful and informative content.
